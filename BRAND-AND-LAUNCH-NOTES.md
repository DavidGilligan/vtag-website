## Business-plan alignment
The hardware is an access point to a vehicle-centred digital passport, not the main value proposition. Focus the public launch on vehicle registration, documents, maintenance logging, mileage, reminders, and NFC access. The business plan describes a passive, battery-free NFC inlay, rounded protective housing, ferrite layer, structural backing and adhesive installation. The plan also references a £13.99 hardware price and £3.99/month subscription; do not publish pricing or lifetime warranty claims until final commercial terms and warranty coverage are approved. Marketplace, dealer and fleet services belong to later phases.

## Trust before launch
Do not promise verified history for all records. Distinguish owner-added evidence from independently verified records. Add final privacy and terms pages, contact details, company information, accessibility testing and verified technical specifications before public launch.
